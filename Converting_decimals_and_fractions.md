1.1 - Write 0.33 as a fraction. (2 marks)

0.33 = (0.33*100)/(1*100) = 33/100

1.2 - Simplify 3/15 (1 marks)

3/15 = (3/3)/(15/3) = 1/5

1.3 - What is the decimal form of 22/7 (1 marks)

22/7 = 3.142857

1.4 - Express 0.123412341234… as a fraction in its simplest form. (3 marks)

let x = 0.1234123412341234...
the pattern repeats after 4 digits so 10^4
multiply x with 10^4 to get 10000x = 1234.1234...
do 10000x - x = 1234.1234... - 0.1234...
9999x = 1234
x = 1234/9999

1.5 - Express 0.828282828282…as a fraction in its simplest form. (3 marks)

let x = 0.828282828282....
the pattern repeats after 2 digits so 10^2
multiply x with 10^2 to get 100x = 82.828282...
do 100x - x = 82.828282... - 0.828282...
99x = 82
x = 82/99
