1.1 - How long will it take to download a 40GB movie with a consistent 2000Mbps internet speed? (2 marks)

40 GB = 40,000 MB = 320,000 Mb. Time = 320,000 Mb ÷ 2,000 Mb/s = 160 s.
2 minutes 40 seconds or 160 seconds

1.2 - If a user downloads that same 40GB movie in two hours at a consistent internet speed, what is their internet speed (In MB)? (2 marks)

2 hours = 7,200 s. 40,000 MB ÷ 7,200 s = 5.555 MB/s. In bits 5.555 MB/s * 8 = 44.444 Mbps.
approximately 5.56 MB/s equivalent to approximately 44.44 Mbps

1.3 - How many times faster is 440 Mbps than 128Kbps? (2 marks)

128 Kbps = 0.128 Mbps. 440 Mbps ÷ 0.128 Mbps = 3,437.5.
3,437.5 times faster

1.4 - What is the cycle time of a bus operating at 120MHz? (2 marks)

cycle time = 1 / frequency = 1 / (120 * 10^6 Hz) seconds = 8.333 * 10^-9 seconds
approximately 8.33 nanoseconds per cycle

1.5 - How many times faster is 320MHz than 16MHz? (2 marks)

320 MHz ÷ 16 MHz = 20.
20 times faster.
