1.1 – What is the purpose of filtering data? (1 mark)

Filtering displays only rows that meet selected criteria, by making it easier to find and analyse specific data.

1.2 – Provide the shortcut keys to add a filter to your data. (1 mark)

Select your data and press Ctrl + Shift + L.

1.3 – What does the Fill colour button do? (1 mark)

It changes the background colour of the selected cell or cells.

1.4 – Provide instructions on how to change the text colour of a cell. (1 mark)

Select the cell, open the Font Colour button on the Home tab and choose a colour.

1.5 – Provide instructions on how to format “1299.99” to “R1,299.99”. (1 mark)

Select the cell, open Format Cells, choose Currency, select the South African rand R symbol and set two decimal places and the thousands separator.

1.6 – Provide instructions for changing your font style. (1 mark)

Select the cell or text, open the font-style on the Home tab and choose the font you want.

1.7 – What keyboard shortcut can you use to convert your data to a table? (1 mark)

Select your data and press Ctrl + T.
