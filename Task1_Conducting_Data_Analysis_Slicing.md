1.1 – In your own words, describe what data slicing is. (1 mark)

Data slicing is selecting a specific subset of a larger dataset so you can focus on specific records or categories so you can analyse them more easily

1.2 – What are the three techniques of slicing data? (3 marks)

- Filtering by applying logical conditions
- vertical sliciing, extracting specific columns in a data set.
- horizontal slicing selecting specific rows in a dataset.

1.3 – Provide instructions for inserting a slicer in Google sheets. (1 mark)

Open Google Sheets spreadsheet and click on the chart or pivot table you want to filter.
In the top menu bar click Data then Add a slicer.
In the sidebar on the right, select the column you want to filter by. Use the slicer’s filter control to choose the values or conditions to display.
