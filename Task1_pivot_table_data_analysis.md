1.1 – Why does management need data before launching a promotional menu? (2 marks)

To understand customer demands and to identify which menu items are most or least popular, so that the promotion targets the right products. so to set prices and discounts that protect profit margins and to measure the promotion's success against a baseline

1.2 – How is profit margin calculated? (2 marks)

Profit margin (%) =  (Profit / Revenue) × 100

1.3 – Which fields would you add to analyse item profitability in a PivotTable? (2 marks)

In the rows add item or product name, for the column section add the values of the sum of revenue

1.4 – Name two business insights you can get from pivot table analysis. (2 marks)

- Which menu items sell the most or contribute the most total revenue
- Which items have the highest or lowest profit or profit margin
