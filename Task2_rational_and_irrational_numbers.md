1.1 - What is the precedence hierarchy in BODMAS? (2 marks)

BODMAS defines the order in which operations in an expression are evaluated, from highest to lowest precedence. BRACKETS(PARENTHESES),ORDERS(POWERS AND ROOTS),DIVISION AND MULTIPLICATION(FROM LEFT TO RIGHT) THEN ADDITION AND SUBTRACTION(EQUAL PRECEDENCE FROM LEFT TO RIGHT)

1.2 - List two rules for writing unambiguous expressions. (2 marks)

 first use brackets to make grouping explicit. second Follow a consistent precedence convention using BODMAS/PEDMAS

1.3 - What is the difference between BODMAS and PEMDAS? (1 mark)

 both describe the same precedence hierarchy. The difference is only in terminology for example orders in BODMAS means exponets in PEDMAS, both give the same results
